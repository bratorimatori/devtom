# Append-Only

*An algorithmic philosophy for devtom.co*

## The movement

Append-Only is a generative movement concerned with **accumulation as an ethic**.
Most computational art is built on erasure: the frame clears, the particles
reset, the buffer is overwritten sixty times a second, and what you see is only
ever the present tense. Append-Only refuses that. The canvas is painted once, at
the beginning, and from that moment forward every mark that lands is permanent.
The composition does not animate — it *accrues*. What you are watching is not a
loop but a record being written, and the record has no undo.

## The computational premise

The algorithm maintains a ruled field of latent baselines, an invisible ledger.
Entries are appended to it one at a time, each a horizontal stroke that grows
from an origin toward a terminus across a measured number of frames, its weight
and extent drawn from a seeded distribution. No entry is ever removed, faded to
nothing, or relocated. The draw call never clears. Density emerges the way it
emerges on a page that has been worked for years: unevenly, in drifts, with
quiet regions the writer never reached.

## Correction without erasure

The movement's central gesture is the **correction**. A completed entry may,
with low probability, be found to be wrong. In a lesser system it would be
overwritten. Here it is annotated instead: a second stroke is appended adjacent
to the first, in a different hand, joined to it by a short tie mark, and the
original is *reduced in weight but never removed*. Both remain legible. Both
remain attributable. The viewer can always reconstruct what was originally
claimed and what superseded it — which is precisely the property that regulated
systems are built to guarantee and that ordinary software casually destroys.

## Emergence and resolution

Because nothing is erased, the system is monotonic: it can only become denser.
This gives the piece an arc rather than a cycle. It begins as an empty ruled
field, passes through a period of visible activity, and resolves into stillness
once the final entry is placed. The end state is not a frame in a loop — it is a
finished document. Two viewers with the same seed receive the same document;
change the seed and the entire history diverges from the first stroke onward.
Determinism is not a technical convenience here, it is the point: an audit trail
that could not be reproduced would be worthless.

## On craft

The restraint is the difficulty. Any algorithm can produce visual incident;
producing *quiet* that still rewards attention requires the parameters to be
tuned rather than chosen. The baseline rhythm, the ratio of corrections to
entries, the alpha at which a superseded stroke settles, the easing of a stroke
as it approaches its terminus — each of these was narrowed until the piece
reads as deliberate rather than decorative. The work should feel like the output
of a system that has been running correctly for a very long time.

## The seed beneath

The conceptual thread is not announced anywhere in the composition. Someone who
has worked inside an electronic health record, a general ledger, or a compliance
log will recognise the behaviour immediately — *correct, never overwrite;
annotate, never delete; attribute everything* — because it is the discipline
their systems are legally required to enforce. Everyone else sees a calm field
of accumulating marks. That is the correct outcome. The reference should deepen
the work for those who hold it, and cost nothing to those who do not.
